Important Information

Clear details before you confirm and pay for your order.

Digital Delivery

Most digital orders are delivered after payment confirmation. Timing can vary according to product availability and support workload.

User vs Full Account

A user account normally provides access to an assigned profile or user. A full account provides account-level access. Password-change permission depends on the specific product and will be confirmed before purchase.

Shopping Orders

For SHEIN and Amazon, send exact links, options, sizes, colors, and quantities. We confirm item, shipping, service, customs, and delivery costs before payment whenever applicable.

Payment

Whish Money is accepted. Never send card numbers, passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive payment information through this website.

Refunds & Replacements

Eligibility depends on the product and issue. Digital codes or successfully delivered accounts may not be refundable. Shopping orders may become non-cancellable after purchase from the external store.

Support

Quick assistance is provided through WhatsApp during available support hours. Response times may vary outside normal working periods.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about subscriptions and shopping orders.

How do I place an order?+

Choose a product or service, press its order button, complete the WhatsApp message, and wait for price and availability confirmation.

How do I pay through Whish Money?+

After your order is confirmed, we send the correct Whish Money payment details. Please do not pay before receiving confirmation.

How long does delivery take?+

Digital delivery is usually fast after payment confirmation, but timing depends on availability. SHEIN and Amazon delivery depends on the seller, shipping method, customs, and final destination.

Can I change the password?+

Only when the selected product permits it. Ask before purchasing because user accounts and full accounts have different access conditions.

What happens if a subscription does not work?+

Contact us promptly with the order details and a clear screenshot. We will review the issue and apply the relevant support, replacement, or refund policy.

How do SHEIN and Amazon orders work?+

Send exact product links and selections. We calculate and confirm the total, send Whish payment details, and process the order after payment confirmation.

Can I cancel a shopping order after payment?+

Cancellation may not be possible after the item is purchased from the external store. Confirm links, options, and quantities carefully before payment.

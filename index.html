How do I place an order?+

Choose a product and plan, then order it directly or add it to your bag. Your bag turns into a ready-to-send WhatsApp message. We confirm availability and the final amount before you pay.

What is a user account vs. a full account?+

A user account normally gives you an assigned profile or user. A full account gives account-level access. Device limits, region, and permission to change passwords depend on the product. Ask us to confirm these details before purchasing.

How do I pay with Whish Money?+

We send the correct Whish Money payment details on WhatsApp after confirming your order. Wait for that confirmation before making a payment. LBP amounts shown on the site are estimates using our store conversion rate.

How long does delivery take?+

Digital products are delivered after payment confirmation, subject to availability and support hours. For SHEIN and Amazon, we discuss the expected delivery time before purchase. Shipping and customs can affect the timing.

What if my subscription doesn’t work?+

Contact us promptly with your order details and a screenshot of the issue. We’ll review it under our support, replacement, or refund policy. You can also sign in to track a support ticket here.

How do shopping orders and cancellations work?+

Send exact product links, sizes, colors, and quantities. We confirm the total including applicable shipping, service, and extra costs before payment. Cancellation may not be possible after the external store purchase, so please check every detail first.
